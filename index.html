FAQ

Quick answers that help users decide — and help Google understand your product.

Does it record across any app?

Yes. It uses a Broadcast Upload Extension so you can record your screen while using other apps.

What is “Say mark”?

In Pro Bug Report Mode, Screen Flow Pro can detect the word “mark” in your narration and drop a timestamped marker automatically.

iOS will ask for Speech Recognition permission the first time you enable this. Audio is processed by Apple’s speech framework to produce the transcript/markers.

What’s included in Pro?

Full-quality exports, advanced workflows/presets, keyword tagging, and Bug Report Mode (transcript + markers). Creator Mode (PiP facecam) is coming soon.

Does the app work on iPad?

Yes — Screen Flow Pro supports iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 16 or later.

How do I share a Bug Report export without losing the transcript + markers?

Bug Report Export produces multiple files (video + transcript + summary/markers). Don’t use “Save to Photos” — Photos only keeps the video.

Use the Share Sheet to send via AirDrop/Mail/Messages, or choose “Save to Files” to keep the full bundle together.
