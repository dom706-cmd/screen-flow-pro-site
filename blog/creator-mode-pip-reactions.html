Creator Mode: PiP reactions (what’s coming)

Watch your recording while capturing facecam — then export picture‑in‑picture. Here’s what matters most.

Why PiP needs real controls

Most “reaction” tools fail for one reason: they treat picture‑in‑picture like a sticker. Creators need control over layout and audio.

  • Position — top/bottom placements that avoid UI overlays.
  • Size + zoom — keep your face readable without blocking content.
  • Audio mix — screen audio vs. mic, with a balance control.

What Screen Flow Pro is building

Creator Mode will play back a Session while recording your front camera, then compile a PiP export. It’s planned as a Pro feature.
