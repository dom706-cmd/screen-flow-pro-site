FAQ

How does Pro work?

Pro is a StoreKit subscription purchased through the App Store. You can cancel any time in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Why does Free export have watermark/downscale?

It keeps Free usable while protecting the Pro workflows that fund ongoing development.

Is Creator Mode included?

Creator Mode is planned and will be included with Pro when released.
